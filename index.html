How the score works

The score is a transparent screening score from 0–100. It rewards low competitor count and high small-company accessibility, while penalizing manufacturing difficulty, litigation risk, and FDA-exclusivity risk. Nearer cliffs receive a timing boost. Adjust the inputs as your research improves.

Competition 30% · Access 25% · Manufacturing 15% · Litigation 12% · FDA exclusivity 8% · Timing 10%
